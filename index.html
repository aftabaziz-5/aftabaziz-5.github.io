About Me

I am Aftab Aziz from District Bajaur. I completed my SSC from FC Public School in 2020 and FSC in Computer Science from Government Post Graduate College Khar. I recently graduated with a Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from Abdul Wali Khan University Mardan.

I have a strong interest in Cybersecurity, Ethical Hacking, and Web Security. During my academic journey, I worked on projects related to API security, vulnerability assessment, and secure web development. My Final Year Project, "CyberGuard," focused on API Security and Threat Monitoring.

I am passionate about learning modern technologies and continuously improving my skills in cybersecurity, penetration testing, and software development.
